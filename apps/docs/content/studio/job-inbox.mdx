# Job Inbox

## Experimental foundation

Open **Job Inbox** (`/jobs`) for the selected project and branch. Durable jobs are experimental. [Database webhooks](/docs/studio/webhooks) now have checkpointed producers and an authenticated executor; alert emails, recovery drills and push campaigns remain separate pending integrations. An empty inbox does not mean these integrations are available.

The queue stores encrypted payloads in private branch tables under the existing single writable owner. Studio and management clients receive metadata only: kind, state, timestamps, attempts and safe error codes. Payloads, secrets and lease credentials are not shown.

Skipped webhook jobs can finish as `succeeded` with a controlled skip code. Inspect `lastCode`/history: `PROVIDER_ACCEPTED` means receiver handoff, while `NOT_AUTHORIZED`, `KEY_REVOKED`, `SUBSCRIPTION_INACTIVE` and `EVENT_EXPIRED` mean nothing was dispatched. Queue success is not proof of user delivery/reading.

## States and attempts

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `queued` | Waiting for its eligible time and an executor. |
| `leased` | Claimed for a bounded lease with a fencing token. |
| `retry_wait` | Failed/expired attempt awaiting another bounded attempt. |
| `succeeded` | Executor recorded success; not proof of downstream delivery/read. |
| `dead` | Retry/deadline budget exhausted or payload unusable. |
| `cancelled` | Future claims/completions are fenced. |

The list refreshes every 15 seconds and supports bounded pagination. Select a job for attempt history; Escape closes the dialog. Viewers can inspect metadata. Administrator-authorized cancel/replay operations are audited and require explicit confirmation in Studio.

## Cancel or replay

Cancellation cannot undo an external side effect already sent by an executor. Reconcile uncertain outcomes before retrying. Dead-letter replay creates a new job with the original event ID and a parent-job link. It is explicit, not an infinite automatic retry.

Replay requires `Idempotency-Key`; keep the same key when retrying an uncertain replay request. A new deliberate replay needs a new key. **Push replay is blocked** until recipient-aware campaign retry exists. Webhook receivers must deduplicate event IDs. Replays recheck current projection/authorization and require the source event to remain inside the 24-hour outbox window.

## Management client and routes

Trusted management code can use `ManagementClient.jobs(projectId, branchId).list`, `get`, `cancel` and `replay`. Keep management tokens server-side; Studio can use its platform cookie. The helpers retain the standard `{ data, meta }` envelope.

| Method | Branch-relative route | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GET | `/jobs?limit=100&cursor=…` | Viewer or higher; maximum 500 rows. |
| GET | `/jobs/{id}` | Viewer or higher; includes attempt history. |
| POST | `/jobs/{id}/cancel` | Administrator-authorized audited write. |
| POST | `/jobs/{id}/replay` | Administrator-authorized audited write; idempotency key required. |

Prefix routes with `/management/v1/projects/{projectId}/branches/{branchId}`. Cursors bind the route, actor and immutable branch. Do not reuse them after switching users or targets. There is no public submission API yet.

## Resource and executor boundaries

Payloads are limited to 64 KiB; a branch permits at most 10,000 pending jobs. Leases default to 30 seconds and cannot exceed 60 seconds. Scheduled eligibility is bounded to seven days. Terminal history/deduplication is retained for 30 days; cleanup does not erase pending work.

Kind policies bound webhook/email attempts to ten within 24 hours, push to six within one hour, and maintenance/drills to three within 24 hours. These are queue policies, not certification of those integrations.

Private internal claim/finish endpoints require immutable project/branch IDs and derive allowed kinds from the executor credential. The webhook executor selects a server-defined subset and cannot consume other work. `QB_WORKER_TOKEN` cannot claim push work. `QB_PUSH_ENABLED=false` is the default; enabling its separate `QB_PUSH_WORKER_TOKEN` only enables a restricted principal, **not a push worker or notification service**. Keep `/internal/*` off the public gateway. See [expanded release status](/docs/roadmap/expanded-release).
